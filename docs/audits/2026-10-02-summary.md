# Harbourline MVP audit — 2 October 2026

Status: CLEAR for the tested offline MVP scope. All filed customer-flow defects were repaired, independently rechecked by their original reporters, and integrated into the workspace.

“CLEAR” means the filed defects and tested scenarios passed; it does not mean every customer-flow combination is robust. The [remaining coverage questions](2026-10-02-coverage-gaps.md) identify unverified recovery, concurrency, model, document and operational scenarios, including concrete source-based recovery concerns.

## Outcome

Three independent reporters audited intake/tasks, billing/operations, and records/documents/signing in separate worktrees and real Postgres databases. A separate fixer implemented the regressions, and each original reporter returned for further fuzzing and verification. Additional findings repeated that cycle rather than being accepted on the fixer's tests alone.

The audit found 48 customer-flow defects: 9 intake, 23 billing, and 16 records. A separate pre-existing TypeScript configuration failure was also repaired. All three original reporters independently cleared every filed customer-flow defect; the integrated regression and browser suites are green.

## Scope and coverage

All 46 seeded playbooks are executable catalogue cases: 29 Hong Kong, 7 mainland China, 5 Macau, and 5 Taiwan. Each case requires completion, its declared document pack, English/Traditional Chinese/Simplified Chinese portal content, and no statutory mutation during document generation. Supported controller updates also require an actual effective-dated row with the confirmed nature and date of control.

Catalogue initial sending and payment use fixtures; separate intake/billing tests exercise delivery approval, invoices and actual payment gates. Signing cases use fake-mail OTPs and real PDF stamping; filing/register changes invoke human approval handlers; external manual actions use staff completion. This is one valid supported shape per playbook, not proof of every legal or free-text permutation.

Browser checks cover intake/manual work, review, tasks, clients/registers, upload/signing, billing/payments, batch, playbook editing, reports and settings. Required widths are 375, 768, 1280 and 1440 pixels, with Chinese content, screenshots, horizontal-overflow checks and page-error checks. This is functional/visual verification, not a production load benchmark.

Every filed defect was first reproduced red against the snapshot or preceding repairs, then made green. Detailed actual run counts, reproduction instructions and histories are in the [intake](2026-10-02-intake.md), [billing](2026-10-02-billing.md), and [records](2026-10-02-records.md) reports. Combined browser execution also reproduced QA fixture leakage, hard-coded database/origin assumptions, and intermittent navigation aborts. The tests now clean their configured fake fixtures and use configured database, storage and URLs. Heavy PDF catalogue cases have a bounded 120-second timeout; business assertions are unchanged. Interrupted or contaminated harness runs are not counted as passing business evidence.
