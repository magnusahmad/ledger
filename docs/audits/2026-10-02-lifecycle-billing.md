## Coverage limits and next verification

Initial red run covers the money/task/card subset of Area 3, not every possible service. Records reporter owns effective-date/person changes, partial signatures, stale upload/sign links, filing/register history and document isolation; avoid duplicate findings. Prior original billing audit cleared invoice numbering/editing/revision, numeric fuzz, races, matching, accounting retries and responsive UI, but that historical audit does not substitute for this lifecycle retest.

The accepted-PDF boundary uses provider acceptance before bookkeeping loss, not a manually changed invoice status. Exact approved PDF bytes exist in the durable delivery journal and Fake outbox captures the accepted receipt; status `draft` alone is insufficient evidence that the client never received an invoice.

The free-request fixtures prove zero fees/disbursements and null invoice on the original request without writing step state. After repair their tail must reach configured invoice lines, current quote approval, real generated/accepted PDF and an unpaid new-service payment gate.

The independent retest above completed these regressions, formerly blocked sequence tails and required language/width UI checks. Authentication/RLS/login and live provider behavior remain explicitly deferred MVP scope. Part-paid revision credits/refunds remain unbuilt; the supported new-service quoting fix must not silently require them.
