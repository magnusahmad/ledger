## Directors

### Appoint a director (委任董事 / 委任董事)

- **Forms:** ND2A
- **Deadline:** File ND2A within 15 days after the appointment
- **Started by:** client request
- **Fee (demo):** HK$1800

**What we ask the client for**

| English | 繁體中文 | 简体中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Full name as shown on ID | 全名（與身份證明文件相同） | 全名（与身份证明文件相同） |
| Chinese name (if any) | 中文姓名（如有） | 中文姓名（如有） |
| HKID or passport number, with issuing country | 香港身份證或護照號碼及簽發國家／地區 | 香港身份证或护照号码及签发国家／地区 |
| Residential address | 住址 | 住址 |
| Correspondence address (shown on the public register) | 通訊地址（會載於公眾紀錄） | 通讯地址（会载于公众记录） |
| Email and mobile number | 電郵及手提電話號碼 | 电邮及手机号码 |
| Effective date | 生效日期 | 生效日期 |
| Copy of HKID or passport | 香港身份證或護照副本 | 香港身份证或护照副本 |
| Address proof dated within the last 3 months | 最近三個月內的住址證明 | 最近三个月内的住址证明 |
| Identity check with selfie (through the secure link) | 自拍身份核實（經安全連結完成） | 自拍身份核实（经安全链接完成） |

**Steps**

1. Send invoice and information request
2. Payment received
3. Collect details and documents
4. Screen against sanctions and PEP lists
5. Prepare resolution and ND2A
6. Send for e-signature
7. File ND2A with the Companies Registry
8. Update register of directors
9. Confirm to client

**Documents**

- Board written resolution: appointment of director
- Form ND2A: appointment, with consent to act
- Register of directors (updated)
- Confirmation to client

**Compliance notes**

- File ND2A within 15 days after the appointment takes effect.
- The appointee signs the consent to act on ND2A itself, so ND2A goes out in the e-signature pack.
- A private company must keep at least one individual director, and individual directors must be 18 or over.
- Complete customer due diligence and screening before the appointment takes effect.
- If the new director will hold over 25% or otherwise control the company, update the significant controllers register too.

4. Check the board still meets minimum requirements

- Form ND2A: cessation
- Register of directors (updated)

**Compliance notes**

- File ND2A within 15 days after the resignation takes effect.
- The resigning director may also notify the Registrar directly using Form ND4.
- Check the company still has at least one individual director after the resignation. If not, a replacement must be appointed at the same time.
- Remind the client to update bank signatories and any authorisations the director held.

## Annual cycle

### Annual return (周年申報表 / 周年申报表)

- **Forms:** NAR1
- **Deadline:** Private company: within 42 days after the anniversary of incorporation
- **Started by:** firm (deadline calendar)
- **Fee (demo):** HK$1200

**What we ask the client for**

| English | 繁體中文 | 简体中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Any changes in shareholders, directors or addresses since the last return | 自上次申報以來股東、董事或地址有否變更 | 自上次申报以来股东、董事或地址有否变更 |

**Steps**

1. Send invoice and information request
2. Payment received
3. Collect client confirmation
4. Prepare NAR1 from the registers
5. Send for e-signature
6. File NAR1 and pay the fee
7. Confirm to client

**Documents**

- Form NAR1, pre-filled from registers
- Current particulars for client confirmation
- Confirmation to client

**Compliance notes**

- A private company files NAR1 within 42 days after each anniversary of incorporation.
- Late filing attracts a higher registration fee that increases with delay, and can lead to prosecution.
- Any unreported changes found during confirmation should be opened as their own matters first.
